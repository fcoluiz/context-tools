# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it with specifics.
- Respect the time of maintainers and other contributors: a minimal reproduction beats a long
  description.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose
behavior is harmful.

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/fcoluiz). All reports are reviewed and kept
confidential.
